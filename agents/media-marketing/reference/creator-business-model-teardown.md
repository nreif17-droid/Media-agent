# THE MODEL BEHIND THE MODELS
**Teardown of Eubank / Fischer / Watson / Fitzgerald / Howe — and your build sequence**

## 1. THEY ARE NOT ONE BUSINESS. THEY ARE TWO.

This is the most important thing on this page. You named five people who look similar on a feed and are running fundamentally different companies.

### GROUP A — Physique-first → low-ticket volume
**Alex Eubank, Liam Fitzgerald**

- **The product is the body.** Aesthetic natural physique, documented transformation, huge reach.
- **Monetization:** app/program subscriptions ($10–30/mo), one-off programs, supplement and apparel sponsorships.
- **Revenue math:** low price × enormous volume. Requires 500k–1M+ followers to matter.
- **Entry requirement:** a physique that is *visually undeniable* in 1.5 seconds. Not "in good shape." Elite.
- **Timeline:** 2–5 years of training before the content works at all.

### GROUP B — Sell the business, not the fitness
**Vincent Fischer, Trey Howe, Wes Watson**

- **The product is a business model.** They teach other people to become online coaches / build income.
- **Monetization:** high-ticket mentorship — $3k–$10k+, sold on a phone call, never priced publicly.
- **Revenue math:** high price × small volume. Works at 50k–400k followers. Faster to money.
- **Entry requirement:** a *result you have produced* and can point to. Wes has prison-to-empire. Vincent has skinny-kid-dropout-to-six-figures. Trey has a sales team and a track record.
- **The hidden engine:** these are **sales businesses with a content faucet on top.** Wes runs closers. Trey runs a door-to-door team. The content generates leads; humans on phones convert them. The posting is the cheapest part of the machine.

**The trap:** Group B is where the money is, and it is the one you are least qualified to enter today. Running Group B right now means selling a $3k mentorship on building a fitness business you have not yet built. That's the specific thing that torches accounts — and it directly contradicts what you said you want your life to be.

**The instruction:** copy their *mechanics*. Do not copy their *offer* until you've earned it.

## 2. THE SHARED SYSTEM — what all five actually run

Strip the personalities away and the same seven components appear every time.

### ① ONE story, told a thousand ways
Every one of them has a single origin narrative and never deviates from it.

- Wes Watson — a decade in prison, rebuilt from nothing
- Liam Fitzgerald — rock bottom, depression, substance abuse, now publicly sober
- Vincent Fischer — scrawny kid, high school dropout
- Alex Eubank — natural transformation, faith, "God saved my life"
- Trey Howe — 9-to-5 and ex-athlete, "1% better everyday"

Notice: each is one sentence, contains a *before* the audience recognizes in themselves, and gets retold in every single piece of content. They are not diversifying their narrative. They found the one and drilled it into the ground.

**Your version — you're currently telling five stories. Pick one.** The strongest available: *"I wake at 12:40am, landscape at dawn, and I'm 17 months out from a Shaolin temple. I'm building myself in public."* Nobody else on earth has that sentence. Own it and repeat it until you're sick of it — that's roughly when the audience starts to hear it.

### ② A visual proof asset
Non-negotiable. There has to be a thing on camera that proves the claim without words. For Group A it's the physique. For Wes it's the transformation plus the raw intensity.

**You do not currently have this locked, and it is your biggest gap.** Two candidates:
- **The physique** — the standard, slower, undeniable path
- **The conditioning** — knuckles, shins, iron-body work, striking endurance. Faster to become visually distinctive, and *nobody in this niche has it.* It also can't be faked, which is the point.

Best answer: build both, lead with the conditioning while the physique catches up.

### ③ One format, absurd volume
None of these people post "variety." They found one or two formats that worked and ran them into the ground for years. Variety is what people do when they haven't found the thing yet.

### ④ A free front door
Nobody sells cold. Every one of them gives away a real thing first:
- Vincent — free training / webinar
- Trey — free Skool community
- Alex — app free trial

The free thing captures contact info. **The follower is rented. The email is owned.**

### ⑤ The DM keyword funnel
The single most copyable mechanic on this list, and it costs nothing:

> "DM me **MENTOR** for online coaching mentorship"
> "DM me **10k** to begin"
> "DM me **SWAT** to join us this summer"

One word, in the bio and in every caption. It converts a passive viewer into a conversation, and the conversation is where money happens. Instagram also weights DM activity heavily — so the funnel and the algorithm reward the same behavior.

Start this on day one, before you have anything to sell. Your keyword right now can point at a free thing.

### ⑥ A price ladder
| Tier | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Capture + trust |
| Low | $20–200 | Volume, buyer identification |
| Mid | $300–1,000/mo | 1:1 coaching — real margin |
| High | $3,000–10,000+ | Mentorship — where Group B lives |

Most people build the low tier first because it feels safest. It's backwards. **The mid tier is where a beginner should start:** ten clients at $500/mo is $5k/mo, and you can reach it with 2,000 real followers instead of 200,000.

### ⑦ Community, not audience
Skool groups, private communities, "Elysium Athletes Club." Retention lives in the community; churn kills subscription businesses. This is also the part that makes the thing feel like the mission you actually described — drawing people's potential out of them — rather than a content mill.

## 5. YOUR BUILD SEQUENCE

### PHASE 0 — Proof (now → month 3)
- Daily content per the 30-day blueprint; find the format that works
- **Start the transformation publicly and put a number on it.** Day 1 photo, weekly updates. The transformation-in-progress is content *and* proof simultaneously.
- **Take 3–5 coaching clients free or at $100/mo.** You're licensed — this is legitimate today. Their results are your only currency later.
- Set up the free front door + DM keyword. Start the email list at zero followers.
- **Revenue target: $0. Deliverable: proof.**

### PHASE 1 — Mid-tier coaching (month 3 → 6)
- Sell 1:1 coaching at $300–800/mo via DM keyword → conversation → call
- Use the exact sales skills you're drilling at 1am. This is where those two lives merge.
- **Target: 10 clients. $3k–8k/mo. This is the first honest money.**

### PHASE 2 — Productize (month 6 → 12)
- Take what you learned coaching real humans and build the low-ticket program ($47–197)
- Free community (Skool) as the front door; low-ticket as the buyer filter; 1:1 as the upsell
- **Target: $10k–20k/mo blended**

### PHASE 3 — Scale the asset (year 2)
- The **fitness app** you're building on Base44 becomes the subscription tier — this is the Eubank layer, and you're already partway into it
- **Ether Bound** launches as brand extension, sold to an audience that already exists rather than to strangers
- **Target: the app and apparel carry the revenue; content feeds both**

### PHASE 4 — The Group B tier (year 2–3, and not before)
Once you have documented client results, a real physique, and a business that actually produced income — *then* you can teach coaches to build what you built. That's where Vincent, Trey, and Wes make their money, and by then you'll have earned the right to be in that room.
