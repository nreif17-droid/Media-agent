*What this account is, what it isn't, and the rules that don't change
week to week. Positioning content lives here; day-to-day content lives in
`content/`.*

## Positioning

**One line:** a man building himself in real time — commission sales by
day, training before dawn, documenting what it actually costs, with a
long-horizon monastic arc in the background.

**The promise to the viewer:** not "I'll make you successful." Instead —
*"I will show you what it actually costs, in real time, so you stop
wondering whether you could do it too."* (kept verbatim from the
blueprint — it doesn't depend on which routine is current.)

**Tone:** ascetic, deliberate, low-ego, a little mythic. Never hype. The
Archmage/Ether Bound aesthetic is real differentiation in a saturated
niche (see below) — lean into it rather than defaulting to generic
gym-account visual language.

**What this account never does:** give advice about something not yet
done (see credibility filter, `CLAUDE.md` rule 2). Never post a specific
workout, result, client story, or number that didn't actually happen
(rule 1). Never launch a product ahead of proof (rule 3).

## Why this angle, given the niche

Per `knowledge/platform.md` and general 2026 research on the space:
fitness/mindset is the single most saturated Instagram category, and the
accounts breaking through in the last ~24 months lean on **authentic,
specific, unpolished documentation over polished generic motivation** —
audiences are fatigued by content that could be posted by anyone. Nolan's
actual life (2:40am wake, commission sales with real rejection and real
closes, pre-dawn training, zero results to fake) is exactly that kind of
material *if it's shot as documentation rather than as a persona.* The
angle isn't "fitness influencer" — it's a specific, verifiable life, on
camera, in real time.

## The four pillars — re-anchored to the current agenda

The original blueprint's pillar examples were written against a routine
that's over (12:40am wake, 1am sales-community calls, landscaping). Per
the MMA build prompt, here is the corrected mapping. **Use this file's
version, not the reference doc's, when in doubt.**

### I. THE FORGE — training (40%)
Unchanged from the blueprint. The physical work — training, conditioning,
whatever the actual movement work is that day. Primary reach engine
because it's visual (see `knowledge/hooks.md` on motion-in-frame-one).

### II. THE GRIND — proof of work (25%)
**Corrected.** Now: the 2:40am wake, the pre-dawn gym session, the DaBella
office-to-field sales day. Commission sales is high-stakes and
emotionally volatile — rejection, a close, a dry stretch — which is
*better* raw material than landscaping was, because it has visible stakes
and variance an audience can follow.

### III. THE DOCTRINE — mindset (25%)
Unchanged mechanically: every post anchored to something that concretely
happened that week, never a floating abstraction. The overwhelm→numbing
pattern — whatever journal or log Nolan is tracking it in day to day —
remains the most valuable material here, same as the blueprint said —
it's the thing the audience is actually losing to.

### IV. THE PATH — the arc (10%)
**Corrected.** No longer a near-term countdown. The Shaolin discipleship
is real but ~17 months out and explicitly deprioritized as present-day
activity — it's the long-horizon background thread, not the lead. **The
lead is what's being built right now**: the sales job, the training, the
Claude Code builds. Use this pillar for the vision/why, not for
counting down days.

## Non-negotiables (full list lives in `CLAUDE.md` — summarized here)

1. Never fabricate. 2. Credibility filter before any advice-shaped post.
3. No premature monetization — proof → 1:1 → low-ticket → app/community →
only then teaching coaches. 4. The 30-min weekday floor is the system, not
the 90-min ceiling. 5. Honest metrics, always. 6. Consistency over
brilliance. 7. Flag scope creep against the other ventures.

## What this account is not

- Not a business-coaching account (that's Group B, teardown §1 — not
  earned yet).
- Not a countdown-to-the-temple account (that's 17 months out and not
  the present-day story).
- Not a "I paid off my life through landscaping and 1am calls" account —
  that routine is over; don't let old drafts or muscle memory reintroduce
  it.
- Not a volume-at-all-costs account — see `knowledge/platform.md`'s note
  that 4–5 quality reels/week is better evidenced than 7/week; the 30-day
  slate is built at that pace (see `content/calendar.md`), and should
  flex down further, not up, if it's not holding.
