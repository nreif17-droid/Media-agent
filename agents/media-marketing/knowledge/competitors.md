# Knowledge: The Five Creators — Reconstructed Funnels

*Compiled Aug 2026. Public info only, no insider data. Every price below is
sourced from public claims (reviews, program pages, self-reported).
Re-check interval: 12 months — pricing and offers shift, but the funnel
*shape* per person is stable enough not to need frequent re-checking.*

For the shared mechanics and the Group A / Group B split, see
`reference/creator-business-model-teardown.md` — this file only adds
sourced specifics under each name, and separates verifiable from claimed.

## GROUP A — physique-first, low-ticket volume

### Alex Eubank
- **Product:** two apps — *Eubank Fitness* and *Eubank Lifestyle*
  (personalized workout/meal plans, live streams, community, monthly
  program updates).
- **Price:** not publicly fixed; his own stated goal is to keep it under
  ~$10/week, i.e. consistent with the teardown's "$10-30/mo" range.
  **[claimed, self-reported]**
- **Verifiable:** two live apps on the App Store/Google Play, both
  actively maintained — that part is directly checkable, not just claimed.
- **Funnel:** reach via reels → app free trial → subscription. No visible
  DM-keyword mechanic as central to his funnel the way it is for the
  Group B creators — consistent with the teardown's read that Group A
  runs on volume, not conversation.

### Liam Fitzgerald
- **Product:** 1:1 coaching + Patreon-hosted programs (e.g. "Hybrid
  Training Program"), sold through his coaching site and Patreon rather
  than a proprietary app.
- **Price:** not publicly listed in available sources. **[unverified]**
- **Verifiable:** 1M+ follower count, 12+ years of coaching claimed on his
  site, sobriety as a stated pillar of his content and brand — matches the
  "publicly sober" note in the teardown.
- **Funnel:** content → coaching site / Patreon, lower production
  visibility into a DM-keyword mechanic than the Group B creators, though
  one may exist and simply not surface in public search results.

## GROUP B — sell the business, high-ticket mentorship

### Wes Watson
- **Product:** tiered coaching, cheapest to most expensive:
  - ~$47–100/mo entry tier ("Non-Negotiable Routine" — structured
    workout/accountability)
  - $299/mo, or $750/3mo, $1,299/6mo, $2,000/yr mid tier
  - $1,500–3,000+/mo "Elite Business and Mindset Coaching"
  - Individually reported cases of $20,000–40,000 for intensive long-term
    mentorship
  **[claimed — pricing pages, third-party review sites, and self-reported
  client testimonials (including at least one "I paid $20k, here's what
  happened" YouTube account) — internally consistent across multiple
  independent sources, which raises confidence above a single-source
  claim, but still not confirmed by Watson himself in a primary source.]**
- **Verifiable:** an actual tiered program structure exists (multiple
  independent listings — Whop, his own site, reviewers — describe the
  same tier shape), which confirms the teardown's read that this is a
  real ladder, not a single flat offer.
- **Funnel:** exactly the DM-keyword pattern in the teardown ("DM SWAT") →
  tier-appropriate sales conversation → the cheap tier is a volume/lead
  business, the top tier is closed by an actual team, consistent with the
  teardown's "sales team behind the content" claim.

### Vincent Fischer
- **Product:** "6-Figure Fitness" — teaches trainers to build an online
  coaching business (app, following, high-ticket closing, $10k/mo
  structure). Also runs a Skool community.
- **Price:** high-ticket program cost **not publicly disclosed** — sold
  via webinar/call, no listed price. Third-party estimates put it at
  $3,000-15,000. **[unverified estimate]** Skool community *is* publicly

  time. **[verifiable — public Skool listing]**
- **Funnel:** exactly matches the teardown's read — free-ish low-cost
  community as the front door/filter, undisclosed high-ticket mentorship
  as the real product, sold off a call rather than a price tag. The
  publicly-priced Skool tier is new information beyond what the teardown
  had — it's evidence the "free front door" is actually a cheap front
  door in his case, not a $0 one.

### Trey Howe
- **Product:** helps "9-5 workers and ex-athletes" start/scale an online
  fitness coaching business. Free Skool community + DM-keyword funnel
  ("DM me 10k to begin" — matches the teardown's quoted line exactly,
  confirming it's still his live funnel as of this research date).
- **Price:** not disclosed in any public source found. **[unverified]**
  The "10k" in his DM keyword plausibly doubles as an anchor number
  (a $10k program, or a "$10k/mo" income promise) but this is inference,
  not a sourced fact — flagged as such, not stated as fact anywhere else
  in this file.
- **Funnel:** DM keyword → free Skool as a qualifying front door →
  mentorship sales conversation. Matches the teardown's description
  closely; no material new information found beyond it.

## What's genuinely new here vs. the original teardown

1. Fischer's Skool pricing is now a **confirmed, public** data point where
   the teardown only had "high-ticket, sold on a call" — useful because it
   shows even the Group B creators use a *paid* (not free) cheap front
   door, worth knowing before assuming Phase 2's "free community" has to
   stay free forever.
2. Watson's tier structure is corroborated across multiple independent
   sources (not just his own claims), which is the strongest evidence in
   this file of any single creator's actual ladder shape.
3. No public evidence found of Fitzgerald or Eubank running the DM-keyword
   mechanic as centrally as the three Group B creators — consistent with,
   and reinforcing, the teardown's core claim that Group A runs on reach
   and volume rather than on individual sales conversations.
