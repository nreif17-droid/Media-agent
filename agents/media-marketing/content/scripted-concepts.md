# Scripted Concepts — Ready to Shoot

Full four-beat scripts (see `knowledge/clip-construction.md` for the
structure) built around the account's real differentiators. Bracketed
text is a real-fact slot, never invented — fill it from what's actually
true when shooting. These correspond to hook-bank rows 49-53. When one
gets shot, log it via `/log-post` and flip its hook-bank row to `used`.

## 1. FORGE — "The Conditioning Nobody Else Shows" (~15s)

- **0.0–0.3s cut-in:** Already mid-strike — hands/shins already in motion
  on the post/bag, tight close-up.
- **0.3–3s hook:** On-screen: *"Day [N] of conditioning bone I didn't
  know I had."* No voiceover needed — raw impact sound carries it.
- **3–13s body:** 2-3 quick cuts across different conditioning work, each
  impact emphasized, text tag per cut ("Knuckles" → "Shins" → "Post
  work"), pace tightening each cut.
- **13–15s close:** Hardest rep, hard cut. On-screen: *"Most people
  train muscle. This doesn't show up on a scale."*
- **Caption:** "Nobody trains for this. That's kind of the point."
- **Why:** motion+stakes in frame one, visually unlike anything else in
  the niche, raw sound = proof. Hook-bank #49.

## 2. GRIND — "Two Ends of One Day" (~20s)

- **Cut-in:** Alarm going off in the dark, hand hits it, already moving.
- **Hook:** On-screen: *"2:40am. This day doesn't end for [N] hours."*
- **Body:** Real cuts across the actual day — gym rep → drive → office
  door → a genuine field moment (a close, a no, a dry stretch, whatever's
  true) — timestamp overlay on each cut so elapsed time does the work,
  not narration.
- **Close:** Walking back to the truck at day's end. On-screen: *"This is
  what funding a dream looks like."*
- **Caption:** "Two lives, one day. Again tomorrow."
- **Consent flag:** if a client's face, home, or door is identifiable in
  the field footage, anonymize/blur it — that's a consent issue, not a
  content one, and it applies regardless of how good the shot is.
- **Why:** quantified effort is a strong forward-to-a-friend trigger.
  Hook-bank #50.

## 3. DOCTRINE — "The Confession" (~15s)

- **Cut-in:** Straight to camera, already mid-sentence — no intro.
- **Hook:** On-screen: *"I numbed out for [X] tonight when I should've
  been working."*
- **Body:** What actually happened, what triggered it, said plainly.
- **Close:** No resolution bow. On-screen (optional): *"Not every night
  ends clean."*
- **Caption:** "Anyone else, or just me tonight?"
- **Why:** vulnerability outperforms polish here — the strongest
  send-to-a-friend format available. Uses existing hook-bank #22.

## 4. FORGE — "Failed Rep, Shown Straight" (~15s)

- **Cut-in:** Already mid-set, camera catches the rep actually failing.
- **Hook:** On-screen, timed to the fail: *"This is what day [N]
  actually looks like."*
- **Body:** The real reaction — reset, second attempt, whatever's true.
- **Close:** Whichever way it lands. On-screen: *"Progress doesn't look
  like the highlight reel."*
- **Caption:** "Didn't get it. Going again tomorrow."
- **Why:** directly tests the strongest single finding in
  `knowledge/hooks.md` — a shown failure over a shown win. Hook-bank #51.

## 5. PATH — "What's Actually Being Built" (~18s)

- **Cut-in:** Already mid-task — laptop, or mid-drive, whatever's real
  that day.
- **Hook:** On-screen: *"17 months from now, none of this looks the
  same."*
- **Body:** Fast-cut montage of the real, current stack — 1.5-2s each,
  plainly labeled ("Selling doors" / "Building the app" / "Training
  before the sun's up") — only what's actually running, nothing padded
  in.
- **Close:** Single still frame. On-screen: *"Not waiting for the
  version of this that's easier."*
- **Caption:** "When will tomorrow become today?"
- **Why:** montage-of-real-stack is the strongest **save** trigger
  available — reads as a trajectory worth checking back on. Hook-bank
  #52; caption reuses #53, already proven on the trial reel.
