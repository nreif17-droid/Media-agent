# 30-Day Slate

*The full structural blueprint — pillar, format, and hook pattern for all
30 posts, planned upfront the way the original blueprint was. What stays
dynamic, filled in by `/plan-week` the week of, is the actual shot list,
caption, and specific hook wording — those depend on what genuinely
happens that week and are never written this far ahead (rule 1,
`CLAUDE.md`). This file is the skeleton; `/plan-week` puts flesh on the
current week's slots and marks them done as they're logged.*

**Cadence — corrected from the source blueprint's daily posting:** per
`knowledge/platform.md`, the evidence favors 4–5 quality reels/week over
7/week. These 30 slots are **posts, not calendar days** — at ~5/week with
a rest-or-stories-only day worked in wherever that week's actual schedule
needs it, 30 posts spans roughly **6 calendar weeks**, not 30 days. If
3+ floor days happen in a row, cut further per `CLAUDE.md` rule 6 — the
slate stretches, it doesn't compress.

**Status:** Post 1 not yet run. `/plan-week` fills in real dates and
content as each posting week is planned, and marks slots `done (DATE)` as
they're logged via `/log-post`.
