# Hook Bank

Running bank, tagged by pillar and pattern. `[bracketed]` text is a slot —
fill with the real specific when using it, never post it unfilled. Status
starts `unused`; `/hook` and `/plan-week` flip entries to `used` and log
the date. When adding new hooks, add to the bottom, don't renumber.

Pattern key: **CS** countdown/stakes · **TA** time anomaly · **CF**
confession · **PR** process reveal · **CO** cost statement · **RG**
refusal of guru frame · **DA** direct address · **IM** in-motion cold open

## Notes

- Patterns to avoid entirely (per `knowledge/hooks.md` and the blueprint):
  "Most people will never…", "The 1%…", "If you're watching this, it's a
  sign…" — not in this bank, don't add them.
- When a hook gets used, `/hook` or `/plan-week` should update its Status
  cell to `used (YYYY-MM-DD)` in place, not delete the row — the history
  of what's been tried is itself useful for `/review`.
- Add new hooks to the bottom as they're generated; keep numbering stable.
