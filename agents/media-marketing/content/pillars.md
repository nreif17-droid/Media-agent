# Content Pillars — Concept Lists

Concrete concept *prompts*, not scripts. Each is a lens to point the
camera through at something that actually happened — `/plan-week` fills
these in with the real week's events, never invents one. If nothing real
fits a concept that week, skip it; don't force it.

## I. THE FORGE — training (target 40%)

- Whatever the day's actual movement block was — filmed as it happened,
  not restaged.
- A single exercise/skill filmed close-up, minimal talking, letting the
  visual carry it (per `knowledge/hooks.md` — motion in frame one).
- A conditioning or striking segment, if that's part of the actual
  routine that day.
- A "day N" running count of a real, currently-active training thread
  (whatever Nolan is actually training toward this month — pull the real
  number from the log, never estimate it).
- A mobility/recovery moment, if that's genuinely part of the week.
- A training failure or missed rep/set, shown honestly — per
  `knowledge/hooks.md`, a shown failure is stronger material than a shown
  win, not weaker.

## II. THE GRIND — proof of work (target 25%)

- The 2:40am alarm, filmed as it actually happens that morning.
- The gap between the gym session and the DaBella office — the literal
  transition.
- A real moment from the sales day: a close, a rejection, a dry stretch,
  a hard conversation. Commission sales has real stakes and real
  variance — use whatever actually happened, not a composite.
- Two ends of one real day, shown together (early training + a specific
  sales-day beat) — only when both genuinely happened on the same day.
- A logistics/food/sleep reality check — what recovery actually looks
  like on this schedule, not an idealized version.

## III. THE DOCTRINE — mindset (target 25%)

**Hard rule, repeated from strategy.md: every post here must be anchored
to something that concretely happened that week.** No floating
abstractions ("discipline is a muscle" is banned as a standalone post).

- An overwhelm→numbing incident from the week's daily log — name the
  trigger, name (or admit not using) the counter-move.
- A response to a real comment or DM from a previous post, answered on
  camera.
- A belief that turned out wrong, tied to a specific week's evidence.
- A fear, stated specifically, without a resolution bow tied on top —
  per the blueprint's "vulnerable, specific, no resolution" rule.
- A real bad day, shown as a bad day — not reframed into a lesson if it
  hasn't actually resolved into one yet.

## IV. THE PATH — the arc (target 10%)

- What's actually being built right now (sales job, training, Claude
  Code builds) and why — the *present* lead, not the Shaolin countdown.
- The Shaolin discipleship as background thread — referenced as the
  long-horizon "why," never as a ticking countdown or a near-term hook.
- What's explicitly being set aside right now (the deprioritized
  ventures) and why — honesty about focus is itself Path material.
- A periodic retrospective: what changed since the last one, backed by
  real logged data, not a vibe.

## Balancing rule

These percentages are a starting default, not a fixed ratio. Per
`strategy.md` and the MMA prompt: **`/plan-week` balances pillars against
actual performance data** (follows-per-1,000 by pillar, once it exists),
not against this fixed split. Until there's ~2 weeks of data, use this
split as the default.
