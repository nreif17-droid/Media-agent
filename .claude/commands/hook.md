description: Generate 5 hook variants from something real that happened
argument-hint: [the real thing that happened]
---

Read `agents/media-marketing/content/hook-bank.md` and
`agents/media-marketing/knowledge/hooks.md` first.

Take the real thing described in `$ARGUMENTS` (a training session, a
failure, a sales-day moment — whatever Nolan gives you). If it's missing
or too vague to hook, ask one short follow-up question rather than
inventing detail to fill the gap.

1. Generate **5 hook variants** across different patterns (CS/TA/CF/PR/
   CO/RG/DA/IM — see `knowledge/hooks.md` for the taxonomy) — don't
   repeat the same pattern twice unless the material only really fits
   one.
2. Prefer adapting an existing `unused` row from `content/hook-bank.md`
   that fits the material over writing something wholly new — reuse the
   bank before expanding it.
3. For genuinely new hooks (nothing in the bank fits), write them, tag
   pillar + pattern, and **append them to the bottom of the table** in
   `content/hook-bank.md` with status `unused` — never renumber existing
   rows.
4. Keep every variant to what the hooks knowledge file supports: text
   on screen from frame one, motion or stakes in frame one, under
   15-25s intent (per the length guidance in `knowledge/hooks.md`), no
   "Hey guys," no name, no logo open.
5. Never use the disallowed patterns ("Most people will never…", "The
   1%…", "If you're watching this, it's a sign…") — they're excluded
   from the bank on purpose.
6. Output just the 5 hooks, numbered, each one line, pattern tagged in
   brackets. No preamble.
